MY STORY & DIRECTION

Learning to turn raw information into insight.

I’m an aspiring Junior Data Analyst with a strong interest in turning raw data into meaningful insights. I’m particularly drawn to the stages that make analysis useful: preparing information carefully, exploring what it contains, and communicating findings in a clear and understandable way.

As a Bachelor of Science in Statistics student at the University of Nairobi, I’m developing a foundation for thinking critically about data, interpreting information and understanding how evidence can support decisions. My academic journey complements my practical learning with tools such as Python, Pandas, Microsoft Excel, SQL and Power BI.

I enjoy exploring datasets, identifying patterns, asking meaningful questions and presenting findings in a way that supports better-informed decisions. I see every exercise and project as an opportunity to strengthen my process: understand the question, examine the data, make reasoned observations and explain the result honestly.

I’m continuing to build my technical and analytical skills and looking forward to suitable opportunities where I can contribute, learn from others and gain practical experience.
